# AI Conversation Video — Overview

## Purpose

This skill defines an end-to-end workflow for producing YouTube videos in which AI characters appear to converse naturally with one another.

The goal is not merely to animate a character. The goal is to make the finished video feel like a believable conversation to the viewer.

## Core idea

Natural AI conversation video is built from three layers:

1. **Conversation design** — character roles, script, timing, jokes, reactions, emotional flow.
2. **Performance design** — lip sync, blinking, head movement, body motion, listening reactions, eye line, pauses.
3. **Video design** — shot changes, subtitles, sound effects, BGM, pacing, framing and export settings.

A technically accurate lip sync can still feel unnatural if the listener is frozen, every line starts immediately, or the camera never changes.

## Recommended production flow

1. Decide the topic and target format.
2. Define each character's role and personality.
3. Generate a dialogue script.
4. Create voice audio for each character.
5. Animate each character using the most suitable tool.
6. Edit the performances into a conversation.
7. Add subtitles, sound effects and BGM.
8. Review pacing and naturalness.
9. Export for YouTube Shorts or standard YouTube.

## Tool roles

### MotionPNGTuber

Use when you need lightweight, repeatable talking-character animation based on a looping MP4 and mouth-shape PNGs. Best for front-facing characters, dialogue-heavy scenes and efficient production.

See: `../motionpngtuber/SKILL.md`

### Hedra or similar character-animation tools

Use when the scene benefits from more expressive face or body movement, larger gestures, or performance generated directly from image + audio.

### Video editor

Use an editor such as Final Cut Pro to combine character outputs, control timing, switch shots, add reaction cuts, subtitles, sound effects and music.

## Naturalness principle

Do not judge quality only by whether the mouth matches the voice. Judge whether the viewer believes the character is participating in the conversation.

Important cues include:

- short pauses before and after lines
- irregular blinking
- subtle idle motion
- nodding and listening reactions
- changes in facial expression
- occasional overlap or quick interjections
- camera changes at meaningful moments
- non-uniform speech rhythm

## Output formats

### YouTube Shorts

- Typical aspect ratio: 9:16
- Common delivery size: 1080 x 1920
- Prioritize a strong hook in the first few seconds
- Use faster shot and subtitle rhythm

### Standard YouTube

- Typical aspect ratio: 16:9
- Common delivery size: 1920 x 1080
- Allow slightly longer conversational pauses and establishing shots

## Guiding rule

The final objective is not "make an animation." It is:

> Make a video in which viewers naturally perceive AI characters as talking and reacting to one another.
